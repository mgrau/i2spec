# Line positions

## Selection rules and branches

For a 0u⁺ ← 0g⁺ transition only ΔJ = J′ − J″ = ±1 is allowed. The lines form an R branch
(ΔJ = +1) and a P branch (ΔJ = −1); there is no Q branch. The hyperfine-free line positions are

$$
\begin{aligned}
\nu_\text{R}(J'') &= E_B(v', J''+1) - E_X(v'', J''),\\
\nu_\text{P}(J'') &= E_B(v', J''-1) - E_X(v'', J'').
\end{aligned} \tag{1}
$$

A line is labeled R(J″) v′–v″ or P(J″) v′–v″; for example, R(56) 32–0 lies at 532 nm. The
hyperfine-free position is the center of gravity of the hyperfine components (see
[Hyperfine structure](hyperfine.md)); it is not itself an observable line.

## The fitted domain

The model gives every line of ¹²⁷I₂, ¹²⁹I₂ and ¹²⁷I¹²⁹I whose levels it holds: X v″ ≤ 108, B v′ ≤ 80 (109 and
81 for ¹²⁹I₂) and J″ ≤ 445, each level bound or held behind its centrifugal barrier. This includes every line of
the line lists but a few hot lines whose upper level the model places beyond its barrier; those keep the
position from the line list's own grid and carry the flag *outside the fitted domain*.

## Units

The model computes wavenumbers in vacuum. Frequencies are ν × 29 979.2458 MHz/cm⁻¹. Air wavelengths
are computed with the Edlén (1966) dispersion formula for standard air.
