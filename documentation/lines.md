# Line positions

## Selection rules and branches

For a 0u⁺ ← 0g⁺ transition only ΔJ = J′ − J″ = ±1 is allowed. The lines form an R branch
(ΔJ = +1) and a P branch (ΔJ = −1); there is no Q branch. The hyperfine-free line positions are

$$
\nu_\text{R}(J'') = E_B(v', J''+1) - E_X(v'', J''),\qquad \nu_\text{P}(J'') = E_B(v', J''-1) - E_X(v'', J''). \tag{1}
$$

A line is labelled R(J″) v′–v″ or P(J″) v′–v″; for example, R(56) 32–0 lies at 532 nm. The
hyperfine-free position is the centre of gravity of the hyperfine components (see
[Hyperfine structure](hyperfine.md)); it is not itself an observable line.

## Correction for the near-infrared bands

The bands v′ = 0 → v″ = 12–17 (755–815 nm) require a J′ dependence of the level B v′ = 0 that cannot
be applied to the level without also changing bands for which no measurements exist. For these bands
a correction of the same form as the level corrections is applied to the line positions directly, and
only within the J″ range of the measurements at each v″. It reproduces the 108 frequency-comb and
beat-note measurements in this region with a held-out rms deviation of 0.13 MHz.

## Units

The model computes wavenumbers in vacuum. Frequencies are ν × 29 979.2458 MHz/cm⁻¹. Air wavelengths
are computed with the Edlén (1966) dispersion formula for standard air.
