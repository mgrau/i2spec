# The Orsay 19 700–20 035 cm⁻¹ atlas and the B state near dissociation

S. Gerstenkorn and P. Luc, *Atlas du spectre d'absorption de la molécule d'iode, Partie IV:
19 700–20 035 cm⁻¹* (Laboratoire Aimé Cotton, CNRS II, Orsay, 1983). This note describes the volume, how
it was digitised, and what it shows about the model in the last 350 cm⁻¹ below the B dissociation limit
(20 043.2 cm⁻¹).

## The volume

The authors re-recorded the (v′, 0) bands with 62 ≤ v′ ≤ 77 because the end of Partie III, recorded for
the bands around v′ = 30, was too noisy there. They used a 50 cm cell instead of 13 cm (p ≈ 0.25 torr) and
summed several interferograms, for a gain in signal-to-noise of about 10 (their Fig. 1).

- **Plates.** 33 plates of about 10 cm⁻¹ each, with a line table: N (tick number), σ (vacuum wavenumber,
  4 decimals), ε (pointing uncertainty from noise alone, mK), I (depth). 3 613 lines with S/N > 1.5.
- **Classification.** Almost every line is assigned: N, σ_mes, I, J″, branch, (v′, v″), σ_cal from the
  Dunham constants of Gerstenkorn & Luc (J. Phys. 46, 867, 1985), and σ_mes − σ_cal in mK. Blends are
  printed as one row per assignment; calculated lines with no observed partner are listed too.
- **Calibration.** The scale is set by a single line, R(26) 62-0, to the absolute value of Pique *et al.*
  (1981): 19 926.0009(10) cm⁻¹, against 19 926.0014(16) in the atlas. The authors estimate the absolute
  accuracy at 0.002 cm⁻¹, and find these wavenumbers 0.0095(10) cm⁻¹ below those of Partie III, of which
  0.0068 is the known Partie III correction and the rest line-shape differences between the two cells.

## Digitisation

Both tables were transcribed by eye from 159 photographs (`data/external/orsay_19700_20035/`), by the
procedure used for the 11 000–14 000 cm⁻¹ volume: full-resolution crops, no digit taken from OCR
(`prototypes/orsay4_crops.py`), each page checked on entry for its structure (`prototypes/orsay_check.py`).
Plates and classification were read by different readers.

The two tables check each other (`prototypes/orsay4_reconcile.py`):

- **Every plate line from N = 7 on is also in the classification**, so each line was read twice,
  independently. σ agrees to 1 in the last digit (the two tables are rounded separately) and I exactly.
- **Every σ_cal reproduces the Dunham constants** (`i2spec.dunham.transition`) to 3 × 10⁻⁴ cm⁻¹, which
  checks each assignment label and σ_cal digit. The 19 residuals of 3–5 × 10⁻⁴ are all P lines at
  J″ = 86–97, smooth in J: the constants, not misreadings.
- **Every σ_mes − σ_cal reproduces the printed o−c** to 1.5 × 10⁻⁴.

Of the first reading, 43 values failed a check and were settled on the photographs
(`data/atlas_lines/orsay1983_pages/resolved.tsv`): 35 plate misreads, mostly glyphs that lose a column in
the dot-matrix print (8 read as 6 or 3, 9 as 5, 0 as 3), and 8 in the classification (6 of them assignment
labels, J or v′, paired with the wrong row on tilted photographs). No genuine difference between the two
printed tables was found. After the checks, every value of σ and I rests on two agreeing readings; ε is on
the plates only, and 100 plate readings and 64 classification readings needed a second look
(`second_looks.tsv` in each directory).

The result: 3 613 lines (`data/atlas_lines/orsay1983_part4.csv`) and 4 024 assignments of observed lines,
2 079 of them to unblended lines (`orsay1983_part4_assigned.csv`), in bands v′ = 51–79, v″ = 0 and 1.
N = 3614 (20 039.2483 cm⁻¹, R(3) 78-0 and P(6) 79-0) is in the classification only.

## What it shows

Median residuals of the unblended lines, in MHz, for a few bands (all bands are in the assigned file):

| band | lines | obs − Gerstenkorn-Luc | obs − i2spec before the refit (i2spec2026j) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 52-0 | 47 | −75 | +150 |
| 57-0 | 86 | −24 | +1 124 |
| 60-0 | 83 | −27 | +3 854 |
| 62-0 | 104 | −35 | −3 388 |
| 64-0 | 98 | −56 | −38 771 |
| 66-0 | 78 | −18 | −109 835 |
| 70-0 | 67 | −3 | −315 530 |
| 73-0 | 44 | −17 | −364 183 |
| 74-0 to 79-0 | 154 | −72 to +30 | no level in the model |

- **The Gerstenkorn-Luc constants hold the atlas to its own accuracy up to v′ = 79**: the median residual
  is within ±60 MHz in every v″ = 0 band from v′ = 53 to 77 (−75 and −91 MHz at v′ = 52 and 51, −72 at
  v′ = 78 on 15 lines), with 40–90 MHz scatter. The v″ = 1 bands sit
  about +100 MHz higher, the same in every band: the constants' X(v″ = 1) is about 0.003 cm⁻¹ low.
- **i2spec2026j was wrong above v′ ≈ 58**, by far more than it claimed (refitted since; below). The error grows from a few GHz at
  v′ = 59–62 to 300–390 GHz (10–13 cm⁻¹) at v′ = 70–73, and rises steeply with J within each band
  (v′ = 62: +0.8 GHz at J = 1, −40 GHz at J = 88). Above v′ = 73 the extended B potential has no bound
  level at all. `lookup.uncertainty` quoted 2 GHz for every v′ > 50, which held to about v′ = 58 only,
  and the line list of `docs/design/near-dissociation.md` above ~19 900 cm⁻¹ inherited the same error.
- **The calibration line nearly agrees with the comb.** At R(26) 62-0 the comb measurement of Goncharov
  *et al.* 2007 is 82 MHz (0.0027 cm⁻¹) above the atlas, just outside the authors' 0.002 cm⁻¹; with the
  two Sakamoto 2024 lines the atlas scale comes out 120 MHz low (below).

## The refit (i2spec2026k)

`prototypes/mlr_b_dissociation.py` refits the B MLR with these lines; `prototypes/orsay4_fit.py` then
corrects each level v′ = 51–79 from the atlas and the comb lines at those levels
(`docs/design/parameter-sets.md`, i2spec2026k). Three findings:

- **The centrifugal term needed its own long-range value.** With the published α(R), extrapolated as
  −8 × 10⁻⁴ out to 20 Å, every band shows the same J trend, +500 MHz at J′ = 0 to −500 MHz at J′ = 80;
  one added constant beyond 5 Å, fitted as −6.6 × 10⁻⁴, removes it and takes the potential alone from
  150–220 MHz to 30–35 MHz (median) at v′ = 51–66.
- **The atlas scale is +119.6 MHz low**, from the fit with the comb lines; the three lines both hold give
  +82, +150 and +138. That is twice the authors' 0.002 cm⁻¹. The potential alone cannot place v′ = 52–62 on the comb lines and v′ ≤ 50 at once to better
  than ~150 MHz; the level corrections do.
- **The full model now reproduces the atlas** to within ±15 MHz (median per band) for every band
  v′ = 51–78, with 13–80 MHz scatter to v′ = 72 and 70–150 MHz above (weak lines, a few mK each). The
  potential binds v′ ≤ 86 at J = 0.

The 70 atlas lines from levels above the asymptote or within 0.3 cm⁻¹ of it were left out up to
i2spec2026m. They are narrow resonances behind the centrifugal barrier (widths < 10⁻⁷ MHz), which the
solver represents once box states are kept out of the v count. Since i2spec2026n they are fitted: median
−6 MHz, robust spread 92 MHz (`docs/research/quasibound-b.md`).
